Order No. 01 — our story

From a 45-seat room in Crestview
to the whole Emerald Coast.

Bamboo was founded in October 2007 by Joe Rice and Danny Ledford after years of working in restaurants in Destin. They wanted to bring real, high-quality sushi and hibachi to the area without the high-end price tag — starting small, with a 45-seat room in Crestview.

Word got around fast. Crestview expanded, then Fort Walton Beach opened, then Niceville in 2015. Three locations later, it's still the same family running the grills — fast-paced, a little loud, and genuinely fun.

“Come in as a customer and leave as a friend.”
